# Sound credits

`asphalt-roll.wav` is a loop mixed from two Freesound recordings, each
dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). It was cut
from their steady stretches, lightened with high-pass filters (250 Hz and
600 Hz), sped up (×1.15 and ×1.35), layered, and crossfaded into a 3 s mono
loop at 32 kHz.

- "skate board rolling" by bruno.auzet:
  https://freesound.org/people/bruno.auzet/sounds/836284/ (110–114 s)
- "Lawn mover on asphalt" by frenkfurth:
  https://freesound.org/people/frenkfurth/sounds/654579/ (100–104.5 s,
  layered 10 dB down)
