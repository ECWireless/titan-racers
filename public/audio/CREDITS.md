# Sound credits

Each loop is cut from a Freesound recording dedicated to the public domain
under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/): a steady
stretch, high-passed as noted, normalized to -20 dBFS RMS, and crossfaded end
to start into a mono loop.

- `asphalt-roll.wav` (3 s, 32 kHz): "skate board rolling" by bruno.auzet,
  https://freesound.org/people/bruno.auzet/sounds/836284/, from 110 s,
  high-passed at 250 Hz and sped up ×1.15.
- `habitat-bed.wav` (8 s, 16 kHz): "ambience_big hangar" by michaelkroz,
  https://freesound.org/people/michaelkroz/sounds/848923/, from 140 s,
  high-passed at 40 Hz.
- `tunnel-tone.wav` (8 s, 16 kHz): "Concrete Hallway with Air Conditioner"
  by ecfike, https://freesound.org/people/ecfike/sounds/474458/, from 40 s,
  high-passed at 40 Hz.
- `stream.wav` (8 s, 22.05 kHz): "stream gentle nearby" by kyles,
  https://freesound.org/people/kyles/sounds/637831/, from 50 s, high-passed
  at 80 Hz.
